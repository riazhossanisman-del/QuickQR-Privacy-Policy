Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuickQR ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our QR code scanner and generator application for Android. QuickQR is developed by Ismanappstudio.

In short: QuickQR does not require an account, does not collect or store your personal data on any server, and all scan history stays on your device. The only data shared with a third party is limited technical/advertising data used by our ad network (Unity Ads) to display ads.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

QuickQR does not require sign-up or a user account. We do not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • The content of QR codes you scan or generate
  • Your scan history
  • Your precise location

2. Camera Permission

QuickQR requests access to your device's camera solely to scan QR codes in real time. Camera access is used only while the scanner screen is active. No camera images or video are recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere — QR decoding happens entirely on your device.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device only, using a local database, and is never uploaded to any server:

  • Scan history (up to the last 50 scanned items), including the decoded text/URL and timestamp
  • QR codes you generate and choose to save or download to your device's gallery (Pictures/QuickQR folder)
  • Your app preferences, such as dark mode setting

You can delete this data at any time from within the app (swipe-to-delete or "Clear All" in the History screen), or by clearing the app's data/storage from your device's system settings, or by uninstalling the app.

5. Advertising (Unity Ads)

QuickQR displays ads through Unity Ads, a third-party advertising network. To show relevant ads, Unity Ads may collect certain technical information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • Device model, operating system, and general technical information
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Unity Technologies, not by QuickQR directly, and is governed by Unity's own privacy policy. You can review it here: Unity Player and App User Privacy Policy .

Depending on your device and region, you may be able to limit ad tracking through your device's system settings (for example, "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in Android settings).

6. Children's Privacy

QuickQR is not directed at children and is not designed to primarily target children as defined by applicable laws (such as COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Data Security

Since QuickQR does not operate its own server and does not transmit your scan content or history off your device, there is no central database of user content to be breached. Locally stored data is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, app sandboxing, etc.).

8. Third-Party Services

Besides Unity Ads (described above), QuickQR does not integrate any other third-party analytics, tracking, or data-sharing SDKs in this version.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add new features or ad partners. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
